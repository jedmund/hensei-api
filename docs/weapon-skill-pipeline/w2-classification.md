**Input:**
- `full_reparse_2026-10-15.json` (a release asset on `catalogue-20261015-sources`), the frozen full-rebuild diff: 67 changed, 10 missing and 26 added versions.
- W3's offline rebuild, `full_reparse_offline_2026-10-15.json` (a release asset on `catalogue-20261015-sources`) (stored sources, no wiki calls). It reproduces all 103 and adds 4 rows on weapons whose wiki page changed on 2026-10-15. Those 4 are classified in [Offline re-check](#offline-re-check-4-new-rows).

**Method:** this classification wasn't re-parsed. Evidence comes from:
- the stored wikitext (`weapons.wiki_raw`);
- the restored catalogue (`catalogue-20261015`): columns, linked rows, timestamps;
- `db/data/manifests/skill_version_gates.json`;
- git history;
- gbf.wiki family and series templates, read in a browser. W3 stores the same pages as `family_template` and `transclusion` sources.

**Check:** on `hensei_pipeline_w2` (restored from `catalogue-20261015-sources`), after the curation is synced, `granblue:skill_parity` reports no differences and `granblue:weapon_skill_curation_drift` is clean.

## Parser cases (for W6a, structure)

W6b confirmed P1–P3 are structure work, not row compilation. The lead is forwarding them to W6a.

- **P1 (`parser`), Illustrious s4 transcendence gate.**
  - **Weapons:** Murcielago `1040711700` "Dos Colmillos II" and Andromeda `1040814400` "Galactic Halo II".
  - **Source:** the stored wikitext has `s4_u1_name=… II` but no `s4_u1_lvl`. `Template:Weapon/Common/Illustrious` fixes `s4_4s_lvl=250`.
  - **Expected:** gate 250/5/5, as for Crimson Splendor II, Ferocious Dominance II and Nue's Calamity II, whose pages set `s4_u1_lvl=250`.
  - **Got:** nil/3/0.
- **P2 (curated; retire once the compiler expands template defaults).**
  - **Illustrious:** `s4_lvl={{{s4_lvl|230}}}`. Applies to Daudaskot, Dos Colmillos and Galactic Halo; expected 230/5/3.
  - **Epic:** `s2_lvl={{#ifeq:{{#var:epic_release_date}}|2019-08-06|120}}`. Applies to the 2019-08-06 Epics: Grand Epic ×3, Heroic Tale, Staff Resonance, Absolute Equality. Expected 120/4/0. Later Epics (All-Might Spear, Hermanubis) correctly stay at 0.
  - Covered today by the `gate` ops.
- **P3 (curated; retire once the compiler expands series templates).**
  - **Source:** `Template:Weapon/Common/New World` injects `s3` "<Element>'s Majesty" (lvl 120, "Small boost to [element] allies' ATK and max HP") on every New World Foundation weapon.
  - **Bug:** pages with their own English `s1_desc` are parsed structurally and lose it.
  - **Position caveat:** today the version sits at `weapon_skills.position` 4 (Sephira Maxi is at 3). The parity key includes position, so a compiler that emits it elsewhere shows missing+added.
  - Covered today by the `add_version` ops.
- **P4 (curated; classifier or W10 correctness, not row-compiler parity).**
  - **Skills:** "Wicked Cool Might", "Wicked Sick Might" and "Unrelenting Might".
  - **Why the parser misses them:** the name parser rejects them because the prefix isn't a known aura.
  - **Evidence they're EX Might:** the icons `ws_skill_atk_a_2_{2,3}` are the family registry's EX Might stems.
  - The `modifier_override` stays. W6b confirmed its `family_curve` reads `resolved_*`, so the override keeps the canonical curve.

- **P5 (`parser`, row compiler, fixed by W6b in #552): Blazing Chain III** (Higurashi `1040913400`, s2 tier 5★).
  - **Source:** `s2_5s_desc`: "…10% boost to special C.A. damage cap, and amplify chain damage by 50%."
  - **Expected:** `cb_amp` static 50 (element allies).
  - **Got:** `dmg_amp` static 50.
  - **Also questionable (W10):** "special C.A. damage cap" is stored as `ca_dmg_cap` 10 in both runs; `sp_ca_cap` looks like the right boost type.
- **P6 (`parser`, structure, W6a): Exo Decimation gate.**
  - **Weapons:** Exo Heliocentrum `1040424300` and Exo Aristarchus `1040618400`.
  - **Source:** `Template:Weapon/Common/Exo` emits the "<skill>: Decimation" tier with `s1_4s_lvl=200` when the page sets `5star_date`.
  - **Expected:** gate 200/5/0, at position 3 like the other Exo Decimations.
  - **Got:** nil/3/0.
  - The other 8 Exo Decimations get their gate from `gate` ops; a compiler that applies `s1_4s_lvl` retires those too.
